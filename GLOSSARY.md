# Christmas Wishlist

The Christmas Wishlist context helps family members share wanted gifts while keeping purchase planning hidden from the person receiving the gift.

## Language

**Family**:
A private group of people who can see each other's visible wishes and coordinate gift planning.
_Avoid_: Group, household

**Family Member**:
A signed-in user or managed child profile that belongs to a family.
_Avoid_: Participant, account

**Admin**:
An adult family member who can manage family membership, invites, and family settings.
_Avoid_: Owner, moderator

**Invite**:
An email-addressed invitation for a person to join a family.
_Avoid_: Invitation link, access code

**Managed Child Profile**:
A family member without a login, represented and maintained by adult guardians.
_Avoid_: Child account, dependent user

**Guardian**:
An adult family member allowed to manage wishes for a managed child profile.
_Avoid_: Parent, caretaker

**Wish**:
A gift request created by the recipient or their guardian for a specific family.
_Avoid_: Item, request

**Gift Idea**:
A hidden recommendation for a recipient, created by someone other than the recipient.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, recommendation

**Claim**:
A commitment by one adult family member to get a wish or gift idea.
_Avoid_: Reservation, purchase

**Recipient**:
The family member whom a wish or gift idea is for.
_Avoid_: Target, giftee

**Surprise Privacy**:
The rule that recipients cannot read hidden gift ideas, claims, or purchase-planning data about themselves.
_Avoid_: Secret mode, hidden view
